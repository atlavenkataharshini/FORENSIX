Guided learning

Keep a clear record of what you know and what you do not.

  1. Preserve the original export; do not edit it.
  2. Record how and when it was collected.
  3. Check timestamp timezones and the source of each field.
  4. Describe observed facts before proposing explanations.
  5. Consider benign alternatives and missing records.

Quick practice questions

What can a SHA-256 match tell you?That the compared bytes match the recorded hash; not who created the file or whether it is authentic.

Do repeated failed logins identify a person?No. They show only what the supplied records contain.

Does a quiet timeline prove nothing happened?No. Missing or unavailable records remain unknown.
