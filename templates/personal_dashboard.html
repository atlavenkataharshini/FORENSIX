Security action plan

If you think an account was compromised, follow official recovery guidance.

  1. Open the provider's official site or app directly.
  2. Change a compromised or reused password; do not follow unexpected links.
  3. Enable multifactor authentication and review recovery settings.
  4. Sign out unfamiliar sessions and review recent activity.
  5. For unauthorized payments, contact the bank/provider immediately and keep transaction references.
  6. Preserve relevant messages and timestamps; do not share OTPs or recovery codes.
